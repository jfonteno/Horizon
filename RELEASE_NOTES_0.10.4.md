# Horizon: Shattered Reach 0.10.4

## Legacy Command

- Reworked the Legacy Archive so Universal, Civilization, and selected Hidden objectives display live progress bars with objective-specific counts.
- Added a prominent private Hidden Objective callout whenever the active civilization has not selected its objective for the current Era.
- Players can no longer submit Orders to end the first Turn of an Era until they choose one of their three Hidden Legacy objectives.
- Clarified simultaneous Horizon Gate overflow bookkeeping: every valid concurrent contributor pays the full Tribute cost and receives personal credit, while the shared Gate total stops at its requirement.
- Disabled and rejected all later Gate Tribute Orders once the Gate is complete.
- Added automated coverage for each Era's Hidden Objective guard, Legacy progress reporting, contributor payment and credit, and post-completion Gate blocking.
