# Horizon 0.34.4: Foundry Currency Center investment

Foundry bots now treat the first Currency Center as a high priority when they lack recurring Currency income. Before the Era deadline, they retain a single Currency toward its two Currency and one Material cost rather than spending it on optional construction, Technology, or an early Gate Tribute. Once two Currency are available, the planner favors the Center. It still allows a Gate Tribute on the Era deadline, or ordinary spending if no owned Habitat has a Center slot. This behavior ends after the first recurring Currency source is established and near the end of the game.

All four challenge profiles passed targeted tests for holding one Currency, building the Center when affordable, and contributing at the Era deadline. An integrated four-player opening with Meridian built the Center on Turn 3 and contributed on Turn 4. Typecheck and 12 AI tests passed. A five-game Medium bot sample did not show an overall performance gain: Foundry averaged 5.8 LP and 0.6 Tributes, compared with 5.8 LP and 0.8 Tributes in the prior small sample. This patch addresses a specific failure sequence, not the wider objective planning and resource scarcity problem. Game rules and save format are unchanged.
