# Horizon 0.10.1

## Map-first vessel command

- All vessel icons now render on a dedicated layer above the hex artwork.
- Select one of your vessels directly on the board to open its contextual actions.
- Move, Establish, Prospect, Forward Scan, and Long-Range Survey guide the player to legal target hexes.
- Clicking a highlighted hex records the Order in the active civilization's private draft without changing public board state.
- Assigning another Order to the same vessel replaces its earlier draft Order.
- Hold is available directly from each vessel's action bar.

## Trade and Diplomacy access

- Market and Diplomacy navigation remains accessible during every phase.
- Transactions and proposal changes are read-only outside Negotiation, with a visible explanation instead of disabled navigation.
- The Negotiation panel includes a direct Trade & Diplomacy shortcut.

## Compatibility

- Existing 0.10.0 saves migrate normally. No rules schema change was required.
