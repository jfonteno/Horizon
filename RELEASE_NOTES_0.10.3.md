# Horizon 0.10.3

## Purchase Command

- Adds a unified purchase catalog for Habitat Modules, Habitat upgrades, Centers, Defense Grids, Combat Units, military carriers, faction vessels, Technology, and Gate Tributes.
- Shows exact Material, Currency, Research, Influence, and Labor costs before an Order can be drafted.
- Tracks projected remaining resources across the complete private draft and blocks unaffordable combinations at selection and submission.
- Unlocks and redesigns the Technology screen around the five canonical branches and their four-level progression.
- Removes redundant vessel movement inputs from the Orders panel while retaining a complete review and removal console.
- Introduces map-driven construction with highlighted legal Habitats and location-specific build options.
- Keeps all construction and Technology purchases secret until simultaneous resolution and makes completed purchases available on the following Turn.
