# Horizon 0.20.2

Horizon 0.20.2 introduces illustrated faction identity and a complete pregame faction archive.

## Faction emblems

- Added the supplied high-resolution emblems for the Varkesh, Helix, Foundry, Aurelians, Meridian, and Farbound.
- Replaced the initial circles in the network scoreboard with faction emblems.
- Preserved text fallbacks for external content packs that do not provide an emblem.

## Faction selection

- Replaced immediate lobby selection buttons with a browsable faction gallery.
- Selecting a gallery card opens a full-screen faction profile.
- The profile includes the faction emblem, two lore sections, strategic role, Homeworld production, abilities, Technology unlock requirements, and Civilization Legacy objectives.
- Players can move between faction profiles without returning to the lobby.
- Factions selected by another seat remain available for reading and comparison but cannot be chosen.
- Current selections and Ready locks remain clearly identified.
- Final selection continues through the authoritative room command and duplicate-faction validation.

## Compatibility

- Existing rooms, saves, Windows hosting, and Cloudflare Tunnel settings remain compatible.
