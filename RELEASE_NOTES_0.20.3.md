# Horizon 0.20.3

Horizon 0.20.3 is a focused map-control and board-geography correction.

## Direct vessel controls

- Owned Exploration, Survey, Deep Survey, Pathfinder, Colony, civilian, and military vessels can be selected directly on the network map.
- Every constructed exploration vessel now has its own map marker and contextual action sheet.
- Legal actions remain available while the room is in Negotiation, allowing players to draft Orders without prematurely ending diplomacy for the room.
- Exploration vessels with public scanning capability expose that action directly in the map context menu.
- Military vessels selected on the map expose movement and CU transfer controls without requiring Fleet Command.
- Only actions that are genuinely illegal because of movement, readiness, location, resources, or rules remain disabled.

## Revised boards

- Standard Board is now a regular side-length-5 hexagon with 61 playable sectors.
- Shattered Reach remains at 61 playable sectors, but four deep void gulfs and narrow approaches make route planning and geography consequential.
- Both maps remain hazard-free.
- Four-player, five-player, and six-player starting sets are explicitly defined.

## Saved-game compatibility

- Existing 91-sector Standard Board rooms retain their original side-length-6 geometry.
- Existing 61-sector legacy Standard Board rooms retain their original geometry.
- Existing Shattered Reach rooms retain the layout on which they began.
- New games use the revised 0.20.3 maps.
