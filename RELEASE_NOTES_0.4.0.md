# Horizon 0.4.0: Trade and Diplomacy

## Added

- Direct offers between civilizations for Material, Currency, Research, and permanent Labor Capacity.
- Economy-based trade range and civilization contact tracking.
- Private hot-seat proposal review with a full-screen device handoff.
- Trade Agreements, Non-Aggression Pacts, Open Borders, Research Agreements, Defensive Pacts, and Alliances.
- Peaceful agreement withdrawal and immediate agreement breaking with its Influence and LP penalty.
- Save migration from earlier playtest versions.

## Automatically enforced

- Trade Agreements and Alliances remove direct-trade range limits and permit Labor transfers.
- Non-Aggression Pacts and Alliances block hostile entry.
- Open Borders and Alliances permit peaceful passage through controlled Habitats.
- Research Agreements discount the first Technology advance each Era.
- Labor Capacity changes take effect at the start of the next Turn.

## Faction support

- The Meridian's first Trade Agreement costs no Influence.
- The Aurelians gain 1 Influence for their first agreement with each civilization and may use Political Capital at Policy II.
- The Helix may sell Technology through Technology Exchange.

Combined defensive combat and Meridian three-party Brokerage remain table-managed because the current digital combat and negotiation models do not yet represent those interactions reliably.
