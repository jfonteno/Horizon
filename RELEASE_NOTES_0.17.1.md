# Horizon: Shattered Reach 0.17.1

## Farbound survey revision

- Replaces the Farbound explorer's short-range Forward Scan Order with one private Negotiation Survey per Turn.
- Allows the Farbound player to select any unrevealed, non-rift hex before sealing Orders and privately learn its tile type.
- Marks Negotiation Survey intelligence as a tradable commodity for the Turn in which it was gathered.
- Preserves the Long-Range Surveyor and its Long-Range Survey Order. Long-range survey records remain in the Farbound archive under the existing rules.
- Enforces survey timing, privacy, repeat-use limits, sale timing, and payment on the authoritative multiplayer server.
- Updates the strategic AI to gather Negotiation Survey intelligence, value it for settlement planning, and offer it without reading concealed map data.

## Interface polish

- Introduces a condensed, technical font stack across the game interface and display headings.
- Enlarges the Horizon logo and command header while preserving the map's available play area.
- Adds the new Farbound survey and exchange controls to local and network diplomacy views.

## Compatibility

- Existing 0.17.0 saves remain loadable.
- External content packs remain compatible with the 0.17 content schema.
