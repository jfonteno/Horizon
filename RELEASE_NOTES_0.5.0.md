# Horizon 0.5.0: Fleet Operations

## Added

- Individually tracked military carriers with class, movement, CU capacity, current CU, location, and readiness.
- Multi-carrier task forces that move together from one origin hex.
- All eight standard military vessel classes.
- Varkesh Corvettes and Xebecs.
- CU loading and unloading at friendly Habitats.
- Recruitment orders for 2, 4, or 6 CU at once.
- One Construction Order per Habitat per Turn.
- Delayed readiness for newly constructed vessels and recruited CU.
- Automatic migration of the former single-Patrol save state into the carrier system.

## Deterministic warfare

- Every CU contributes exactly 1 CS and military vessels contribute 0 CS.
- The stronger force survives with CU equal to the strength difference.
- Equal forces destroy one another.
- Multiple friendly carriers may combine into one task force.
- Habitat defense includes stationed CU and either 1 or 3 Home Guard CS.
- Assault Vessels reduce defending Home Guard by 1 CS.
- Captured Colonies and Metropolises normally lose one tier.
- Excess Centers are removed when capture reduces available Center slots.
- Varkesh Habitats retain their tier through Occupation Doctrine.
- Varkesh Glory in Battle awards are limited to once per opponent per Turn.

## Rules boundary

The locked current playtest rules state that there are no retreats. Version 0.5.0 therefore resolves defeated forces without a retreat step. Secret simultaneous orders, opposing edge movement, and complete multi-civilization allied defense remain table-managed until Horizon receives its full order-resolution phase.
