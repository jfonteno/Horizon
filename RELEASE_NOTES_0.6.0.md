# Horizon 0.6.0: Scoring and Endgame

## Added

- A dedicated Legacy Archive for Universal, Civilization, and Hidden objectives.
- All 12 Universal Legacy objectives across the four Eras.
- All 18 Civilization Legacy objectives for the six base factions.
- All 40 private Hidden Legacy cards, divided into four Era decks.
- Private hotseat handoff before viewing and selecting a Hidden objective.
- Automatic scoring checks for game state Horizon can verify directly.
- Assisted confirmation for objectives that depend on table-level negotiation or faction systems not yet represented digitally.
- A visible LP breakdown by Universal, Civilization, Hidden, faction, and manual scoring.
- Automatic Hidden Legacy scoring at each Era boundary.
- Horizon Gate success or collective defeat resolution at the end of Turn 16.
- Final standings with the four locked tiebreakers and shared victory support.

## Tracking coverage

The playtest now records Habitat establishment and capture, Metropolis capture, exploration, production types and totals, Technology advances, trade partners and transfers, agreements, Labor trades, Center and vessel construction, CU recruitment, combat initiation, Market income, original Habitat ownership, and Gate Tributes.

## Compatibility

Version 0.6.0 migrates saves from earlier patches. Existing LP is retained as Manual LP when the older save does not contain a scoring breakdown. All Trade, Diplomacy, Fleet Operations, map, faction, and visual-package systems remain available.
