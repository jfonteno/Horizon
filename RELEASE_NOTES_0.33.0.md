# Horizon 0.33.0

## Closed Beta Multiplayer Foundation

- Consolidates room creation into a single configuration page with format, map, expansion, and seat options.
- Adds an account-gated Faction Selection lobby with visual seats, host seat controls, and Random bot factions.
- Lets invited players claim the next open human seat, choose an available faction, and recover the room from another device.
- Keeps bot and human seats interchangeable in the lobby and during paused games.
- Adds server-authoritative, idempotent match settlement for eligible multiplayer games.
- Adds Commander statistics for games, wins, Gate outcomes, Legacy, Tributes, trades, and combat wins.
- Unlocks each faction emblem after three eligible victories with that faction and allows the player to equip it.
- Preserves invitation intent through Commander account creation, recovery, and supported provider sign-in.

Academy games, solo games, local games, and matches with fewer than two account-backed human commanders do not affect multiplayer statistics or achievements.
