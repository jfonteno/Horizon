# Horizon: Shattered Reach 0.19.0

## Complete Network action surface

- Adds a dedicated Horizon Gate panel with contribution progress, costs, eligibility, and private Tribute drafting.
- Adds the System Market with every legal resource exchange and current Technology-adjusted rates.
- Adds the Meridian Economic Salvage operation.
- Restores the complete Legacy archive with Hidden, Universal, and Civilization objectives, live progress, automatic scoring status, and manual claim controls.
- Adds Foundry Prospect and Farbound Long-Range Survey Orders to vessel actions.
- Restores the Military panel in the local interface.

## Clear map and Order workflow

- Shows all available actions after selecting a player-owned vessel.
- Explains why Establish Habitat or Prospect is unavailable instead of presenting a silent or inert control.
- Sends drafted vessel actions directly to the private Orders review.
- Confirms Build, Technology, movement, Gate, and faction Orders in the Network interface.
- Preserves unsealed private Order drafts in the current browser across refreshes.
- Adds Network map zoom, centering, targeting instructions, and target cancellation.
- Adds accessible vessel labels and visible selected-vessel state.

## Responsive navigation

- Keeps every primary gameplay panel reachable from a persistent mobile action bar.
- Removes the height cap and nested scrolling from the mobile action rail.
- Keeps economy, objective, and vessel controls usable at phone widths.

## Compatibility and verification

- Existing 0.17.x and 0.18.x rooms remain compatible.
- Native Windows hosting and the esbuild 0.18.20 lockfile repair remain included.
- The Turn timer and lobby interaction improvements from 0.18.3 remain included.
- Automated parity checks require every sealed Order category and major immediate command to remain reachable in Network play.
- No Cloudflare Tunnel changes are required.
