# Horizon 0.33.12

## Wayfarer Mobile Extraction

- Deployed Arkships, Grand Arks, and Worldships each produce 1 resource matching the Material, Currency, Research, or Influence world beneath them during Production.
- Extraction does not require a Center and does not require ownership of the world.
- Each eligible mobile Habitat extracts independently, including multiple mobile Habitats occupying the same world.
- Resource IV does not double automatic extraction. It continues to double economic mobile Centers normally.
- Undeployed mobile Habitats and ships on Homeworlds, Labor worlds, anomalies, rifts, or empty sectors do not extract resources.

## Interface and Training

- Selected-hex Production details identify each mobile Habitat's combined extraction and mobile Center output.
- The Wayfarer faction dossier and Horizon Academy guidance explain Mobile Extraction as a separate source of production.

## Bot and Legacy Support

- Bot production forecasting now includes both automatic extraction and mobile Center output.
- A resource world used for extraction is recorded for Wayfarer production-location Legacy objectives even when the mobile Habitat has no Center.

## Compatibility

- Existing rooms, accounts, invitation codes, saves, and mobile Habitats remain compatible.
- The new production rule applies automatically to existing deployed Wayfarer mobile Habitats after the server is updated.
