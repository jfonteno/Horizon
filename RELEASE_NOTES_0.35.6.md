# Horizon 0.35.6 playtest patch

- Automatic Universal Legacy objectives now award every civilization that qualifies in the same evaluation. The objective closes after the shared award, and its card names every claimant. Manual claims require the objective to be complete.
- At the beginning of each Era, the Hidden Legacy screen shows all three Policy cards and requires human players to seal a vote or explicitly abstain before sealing Orders. Votes are private until resolution, and committed Influence is deducted immediately. Bot Policy votes still resolve with the same election.
- Combat and bombardment hexes retain a visible red border by reading the saved turn replay history, including earlier turns.
- Mobile vessel actions take priority over the game drawers and order bar; a disabled Move action now explains when its vessel becomes ready or has used its movement.

Existing room and commander data are preserved by the standard self-hosted update process. The game export used to diagnose this issue is not included in the release.
